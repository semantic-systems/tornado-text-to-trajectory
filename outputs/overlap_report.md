## Limitations

1. **The GDELT side is only what our queries returned**, not the whole index; level 2 is what quantifies that difference.
2. **Title matching is fuzzy.** Desk edits, translated headlines and truncation all reduce similarity; both a Jaccard and a containment score are recorded per pair so a reviewer can see which carried the match.
3. **The source→domain map is incomplete** for outlets that never print a URL; it currently covers 66% of articles. Unmapped outlets can still match on a strong headline agreement alone.
4. **The LexisNexis corpus is one query's complete export** — 22,896 unique articles, not truncated — but it is not a census of tornado coverage: it is bounded by which outlets LexisNexis indexes and by the query terms. These shares therefore describe this corpus, not news coverage of tornadoes in general.
