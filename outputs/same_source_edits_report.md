# Same-source edits among the official texts

Tag `2010-01-01_2025-12-31`. Generated by `scripts/25_find_same_source_edits.py`; the method is described in `src/tornado_dataset/text/same_source.py`.

Which official texts of one hazard (survey statement, Storm Events narratives, DAT comments, LSRs) are edits of one account rather than independent descriptions. A shared run of 5 tokens is *specific* when it occurs in the texts of at most one hazard besides the pair's own (each text counted for one hazard: an LSR for its best match, stage 20); two texts are linked when at least **10** of their tokens lie in specific runs they share. Linked texts of a hazard form a group.

## 1. Calibration

Pairs that cannot be copies stand in for the false links, without hand labels: each text of a within-hazard pair is paired with a random text of the other's source type and office, either from **another episode** of the same year (no copy, no shared topic: a lower bound) or from **the same episode, another hazard** (same day, office and templates, but also some real copies between pieces of one tornado and through LSRs linked to the wrong tornado: an upper bound; identical texts excluded). Rule: the smallest number of shared tokens from 10 at which at most 0.1% of the first and 1% of the second would link. Result: **10** (calibrated).

## 5. Scope and limits

- Verbatim reuse only. A narrative that retells the survey in new words counts as a separate account; section 2 shows how detection fades with rewording.
- No origin is inferred. Narratives and DAT comments carry no time, an LSR's time is the event's, and each hazard has one primary survey statement, so no pair has two publication times. `contained_in` and the representative are observations; the representative's tie-break (PNS, narrative, DAT comment, LSR) is a convention.
- Groups are per hazard. A text linked to two hazards is grouped separately for each.
- Each hazard's primary survey section is compared. Earlier revisions of it are stage 20 `mentions` and are not compared.
